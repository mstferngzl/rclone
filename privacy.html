Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This application is a personal tool used only by its owner to sync files between their own computer and their own Google Drive account using rclone.

  • It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive data.
  • No data is collected, stored, shared with or sold to any third party.
  • Files are transferred directly between the owner's computer and Google Drive. No other server is involved.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
